# Billing, credits and invoices

Billing tables, rating, collect, reconcile, credits, invoices and the price-list admin. Verbatim items of the former single AGENTS.md. Add a new trap here as a `- **Title.** ...` item and put one title line for it into the index in AGENTS.md.

- **Organizer billing lives in the tables of migration 0157 (billing wave B-A, task B1-1), and `tariffs` is LEGACY (read-only, marked by a table comment).** Rates are ROWS, never Go constants: `price_lists` (versioned per `code`+`currency`, the version in force at a sale is the latest `effective_from <= occurred_at` not revoked, `gen.GetPriceListVersionAt`), its `price_list_rules` (`rule_type` + `params` jsonb with minor units and `pct_bp` basis points) and `price_list_packs`; an organization's individual rate is `org_rate_overrides` and replaces the price list everywhere. A number changes by a NEW price-list version, never an UPDATE; the seed `standard-2026` (EUR and USD, from 2026-01-01) carries the policy numbers that still wait for section 12 of `docs/pricing/pricing_policy_2026-10-16_ru.md`. An account is `billing_accounts` (organization, currency, kind) with dated `billing_account_terms`; the migration created one per currency of each live organization's venues through `billing_country_routes` (a country without a route bills in EUR), and `billing.EnsureAccount` applies the same rule to later organizations. Both `billing_accounts.org_id` and `org_rate_overrides.org_id` reference `organizations` WITHOUT cascade on purpose, so a fixture that hard-deletes an organization deletes those rows first. `billing.purchase` and `billing.payer_manage` are owner-only and in `apikeys.ForbiddenScopeExact`. `price_lists.bonus_share_cap` is numeric and is read as basis points (`BonusShareCapBP`), never a float.

- **Three static billing guards run before the billing tables exist (B1-3, `tests/staticanalysis/billing_append_only_test.go`, scanners in `billing_guard_scan_test.go`, fixtures in `testdata/billing_guard/{good,bad_*}`).** (1) `billable_events`, `charges`, `charge_reversals`, `credit_ledger`, `campaign_grants` are INSERT-only: any `UPDATE`/`DELETE FROM`/`TRUNCATE` of them under `apps/backend/{cmd,internal,tests}` fails, even inside `internal/platform/billing` (a correction is a compensating row); the only exceptions are `_test.go` files, testdata and a migration's Down section (a migration's Up section is scanned too). (2) `INSERT INTO` any of those five plus `charge_allocations`, `credit_lots` is allowed only in migrations, `queries/billing_*.sql`, `gen/billing_*.sql.go` and `internal/platform/billing/**`, and a non-test Go file outside the package (and outside `gen/billing_*`) may not call a wrapper matching `.(Insert|Append)(BillableEvent|Charge|ChargeReversal|ChargeAllocation|CreditLot|CreditLedger|CampaignGrant)…(`, so name new write wrappers that way and put their SQL in `queries/billing_*.sql`. (3) In `gen/billing_*.sql.go` and `internal/platform/billing/**` (non-test) no `float32/64` in a struct field, parameter or result, every field named `…Minor|Amount|Total|Price|Face|Remaining|Rate` is `int64`/`*int64` (`…Bp` is exempt, it is not matched) and its struct has a `Currency string` — a plain count field named `Total` in that package trips it too, rename it (`Count`). A new allowed place goes into the allowlist helpers (`isBillingPackageFile` etc.) in `billing_guard_scan_test.go` with a fixture, never by weakening a regexp. Comments are blanked before matching, so prose may mention the tables; fixture Go files are `*.go.txt` so go vet and gofmt ignore them.

- **Rating is ONE pure function, `billing.Rate(terms, event)`, over terms loaded "at the date" by `billing.TermsAt(ctx, q, accountID, occurredAt)` (task B1-2, `internal/platform/billing/{rules,terms,rate}.go`).** `TermsAt` takes the terms row, the price-list version (in the ACCOUNT's currency), its rules and packs and the individual rate all at `occurred_at`, never at the time the job runs, and parses every rule (`ParseRule`: unknown type, unknown or missing key, a fraction, a negative amount, basis points above 10000, two rules of one type for the same line/event/sale currency are `ErrBadRule`, never a zero rate). A `Terms` is bound to its `At`: `Rate` refuses an event with another `OccurredAt` (`ErrInconsistentEvent`), so a job calls `TermsAt` per event. Rounding is integer floor of `amount × bp / 10000` in minor units (as `hcheckout.ComputePricingLines`). Order: refund/cancel/session_cancelled = 0 `journal_only` (the cancelled-session reversal is the job's, B2-3); complimentary = 0 `free`; price after promo <= 0 = `free`; an individual rate (`org_rate_overrides`) replaces the price list EVERYWHERE, low-price formula and venue percentage included (run decision R-B6); venue plan = `percent_of_price_with_cap` on price minus discount, a sale in another currency is `Deferred = fx_rate_missing` (no exchange rate in code, R-B5); class `low_price` = `low_price_formula` (a sale in another currency than the account's is deferred with `fx_rate_missing` too); else `fixed_per_ticket`. Every percentage is rounded DOWN (floor), rule params are bounded by 1e9 minor units, and the price list code must equal the one the terms name. `ClassifyLowPrice` (for collection, B2-2) compares the price BEFORE the promo code with the threshold of the low-price rule for the SALE currency; no rule for that currency = `standard` with `resolved=false`. `volume_above_baseline`, `subscription`, `generator_run` parse but a line carrying one fails with `ErrRuleNotImplemented`. A struct field ending in Price/Rate/Amount/Minor is read as money by the B1-3 guard (hence `Rule.LowPriceFormula`, not `LowPrice`), and every struct with one needs `Currency string` (`Event.Currency` is the SALE currency, `Charge.Currency` the account's).

- **`GET /v1/billing/tariffs/active` reads the EFFECTIVE rate of the organization's billing account, not the legacy `tariffs` table (B1-4, `hbilling/tariff_compat.go`).** The JSON keys are still `TariffToResponse`'s (openapi untouched): `per_ticket_fee_minor` is the individual rate's unseated rate (`plan_name` `individual`) else the standard line's `fixed_per_ticket` unseated rate (0 on a venue plan, the share is in `notes`), `per_event_fee_minor` a `fixed_per_event` rule or 0, `monthly_fee_minor` always 0; the seated rate has no field. No `org_id` = the `standard-2026` EUR list; an organization with no account/terms/price list falls back to its `tariffs` row. Because an answer may now be an individual rate, `?org_id=` goes through `rowOrgAccess` (404 for an outsider, 503 unguarded) - the route is `flatRowGuard` in the SEC-3 table. `POST /v1/billing/tariffs` and invoice generation still use `tariffs` until block B4, so a tariff posted there no longer shows in `active` for an organization that has an account.

- **The price-list screen is `/v1/admin/billing/*` (task B1-5, `httpserver/hbilling/admin*.go`, admin-web `/billing/prices`), and its rules are the money guard rails, not conveniences.** Every route needs `billing.admin` AND the platform-superadmin marker (`requireBillingSuperadmin` in `mount_admin.go`, 403 `billing.superadmin_only`; the platform `admin` role also holds `billing.admin`, so the permission alone is not enough) and every write needs `X-Admin-Reason`. Every write writes its audit row on the CHANGE'S OWN transaction through `AdminHandler.auditTx` and answers 503 when there is no audit writer; `hbilling/admin_audit_guard_test.go` (source scan: a `HandleCreate/Revoke/Close/Delete*` that does not call `auditTx` before `Commit` fails) and `billing_admin_audit_guard_test.go` (a new write route needs an action in `billingAdminWriteRoutes`, and the integration test must see that action in `audit_events`) keep it so. A price-list version is only INSERTED (next number of its `(code, currency)` series, revoked ones counted); the one UPDATE is `revoked_at`, and only before `effective_from` (`RevokePriceListVersion` repeats it in its WHERE, 409 `billing.price_list_in_force` afterwards; 409 `billing.price_list_in_use` for the first version accounts depend on). A new version, individual rate and terms row must START IN THE FUTURE and after the latest of their series (price list: latest non-revoked version; rate: latest of the organization+currency, and it ENDS the open one at its own start; terms: latest of the account), so none reaches an event that already happened. An individual rate is closed only for the future (`effective_to` > now) and removed only before it starts. Rules are checked by the rating engine's own parser (`billing.CheckRuleInput`/`CheckRuleSet` over `ParseRule`) plus the (type, line, event kind) pairs `Rate` actually reads; a type `Rate` cannot compute yet is refused; a rule the previous version had and the new one lacks is a WARNING in the answer, not a refusal. Terms are refused when the price list has no version in force at their start or lacks the rule the plan is rated by (`fixed_per_ticket` standard, `percent_of_price_with_cap` venue), and a tools account is prepaid only. There is NO default number in the API or the screen: every rate, cap and threshold is typed by the operator or copied from an existing version ("Start from"). Display flags are "permanent venue" (plan kind of the terms in force), "tools" (`account_kind`) and "existing client" = the organization has any individual rate: the organization-level `billing_legacy_since` of spec 41 section 5.5 is NOT in migration 0157, so a real "existing client" date still needs a migration. Dates in the form are read as UTC. A test that must show "after the start" inserts the past version by SQL (the screen never writes a start in the past).

- **The price-list admin (Б1-5) locks the SERIES before the row and judges "in the future" after the lock (review 2026-10-17).** `HandleRevokePriceList`, `HandleCreatePriceList` and `HandleCreateAccountTerms` all take `LockPriceListSeries(code, currency)` first and the version row second (a revoke reads code and currency unlocked, locks, then re-reads the row), so two parallel revokes cannot leave an account with no covering version and a revoke cannot race a new terms row. `h.now()` is read AFTER the lock in every create, revoke, close and delete: a long lock wait must not let a start time slip into the past. Closing an individual rate never extends its period (`SetOrgRateOverrideEnd` requires the new end before the stored one); deleting a not-yet-started rate does NOT give its predecessor's `effective_to` back, and a terms row is checked only against the version in force at its `effective_from`, not against later versions (`droppedRuleWarnings` only warns). Admin writes need an audit writer for which `audit.IsDurableInTx` is true (`PGWriter`, wrapped by `WithServiceActor`/`WithVia`); `SlogWriter.WriteTx` only logs, so a route that promises "audit row and change commit together" answers 503 with it. A new `audit.Writer` wrapper must implement `DurableInTx`.

- **The billing event store (B2-1, migration 0158) is idempotent by `(kind, subject_id)` and "rated" means "has a DIRECT charge".** `gen.InsertBillableEvent` is `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` and answers `inserted=false` on a repeat, so the collect job may rescan a window (its 10-minute overlap) freely. `charges` has two PARTIAL unique indexes, `charges_one_direct_per_event_uq` (`WHERE reversal_of IS NULL`) and `charges_one_reversal_per_charge_uq`, so `gen.InsertCharge` fails with 23505 on a second rating or a second reversal and the rate job reads that as "already done". An event that costs nothing (refund, cancellation, free ticket) still gets a zero-amount direct charge with a `reason`, because the append-only event row has no "rated" marker and `ListUnratedBillableEvents` is simply "no direct charge". A reversal row points at the ORIGINAL event (sums per event net to zero), the cause event lives in `charge_reversals.cause_event_id`; direct amounts are >= 0 and reversals <= 0 by CHECK. `ListChargesByAccountPeriod` and `SumChargesByAccountPeriod` filter by the EVENT's `occurred_at`, so a reversal counts in the period of the sale it reverses. `event_id/session_id/order_id/ticket_id` on `billable_events` have no foreign keys on purpose, and `org_id`/`account_id` have no cascade: a fixture that hard-deletes an organization deletes `charge_allocations`, `charge_reversals`, `charges`, `billable_events` and `billing_accounts` first (test files may DELETE them, the B1-3 guard forbids it anywhere else).

- **Migration 0158 also adds the time-window indexes the collect queries need** (`orders (paid_at)`, `refunds (settled_at)`, `tickets (cancelled_at)`, `tickets (issued_at) WHERE complimentary_issuance_id IS NOT NULL`, `event_publications (published_at)`, `session_changes (created_at) WHERE 'cancelled' = ANY(kinds)`, all partial where the column is nullable). Proof on the 12k-order dev copy: a one-day `ticket_sold` pass went from `Seq Scan on order_items` + `Seq Scan on orders` to index scans on both. The small tables (refunds, session_changes, event_publications) still show a seq scan there because they hold 0-111 rows; with `enable_seqscan = off` each plan uses its new index. A collect query must repeat the index predicate verbatim (`'cancelled' = ANY(kinds)`, `paid_at IS NOT NULL`) or the partial index is not eligible.

- **`billing.collect` (B2-2, `internal/platform/billing/collect`) writes billing facts from the sales tables and only runs once the owner names the date X.** `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE` (RFC 3339, arena-worker only, empty = off: nothing is seeded and the handler does nothing) is the lower bound of EVERY source; sales before it are never read. Seven sources, each a keyset page over `(occurred_at, subject_id)` in `gen/billing_collect.sql.go`: `ticket_sold` (paid `order_items` x `orders.paid_at`; seated = `session_seats.kind = 'seat'`, class from `billing.ClassifyLowPrice` on the unit price BEFORE the promo; a currency with no threshold rule stays `standard` with attribute `low_price_unresolved`; terms missing at that date add `terms_unavailable` and the rate job fails visibly later), the same lines of `source='complimentary'` orders as `ticket_complimentary`, `ticket_refunded` (succeeded refunds, timed `COALESCE(succeeded_at, created_at)` - NEVER `settled_at`, which the repair pass stamps later; indexed by `refunds_succeeded_time_idx`), `ticket_cancelled` (cancelled/revoked tickets that no SUCCEEDED refund with `cancel_ticket` cancelled: a failed refund does not hide the cancellation, and `tickets.refund_id` is not the rule because it is linked best effort and points at refunds in any state), `session_cancelled` (`session_changes.kinds` contains `cancelled`), `event_published` (the MINIMUM `event_publications.published_at`: `events` has no `published_at`) and `ticket_complimentary` (invitation tickets with no order). The account is `billing.EnsureAccount(org, venue country)`, falling back to the organization's `legal_address_country`, else `country`. Cursors live in `billing_job_cursors` under `collect.<source>`: a DRAINED source stores the pass start minus 10 minutes (a late-committed row is read again, the write is idempotent); every full page stores its last time, never past that horizon; a failing row stops ONLY its source (the others still run, `Run` returns the joined errors) and leaves the cursor at the last full page. Known limits: more than 10,000 rows at one single instant freeze that source's cursor (a warning is logged, the writes stay idempotent); a row that commits more than 10 minutes late is missed (the reconcile job B2-5 finds it); lowering the date X after a pass does not re-read the gap; `event_published` is the minimum of the CURRENT publications, so unpublishing every feed and publishing again moves it; `session_cancelled` is one event per journal row (a repeated cancel row is a second event, the reversal is unique per charge); a date X before 2026-01-01 is refused. The first pass of a source labels its events `backfill`. The handler schedules the next pass BEFORE the work, so a pass that fails all retries does not end the chain, and `PGScheduler` never inserts a second pending job. The collector uses pool-bound queries and no transaction, and it lives under `internal/platform/billing/**` because the Б1-3 guard only lets that tree call the `Insert…BillableEvent` wrapper. **An integration test of it must scope the store to the fixture's organization (see `scopedStore`) and use its own `CursorPrefix`**: the shared database holds other packages' orders, and a billable event pins its organization with a foreign key, so an unscoped collector would both write facts for strangers and block their fixtures' organization delete. Not done here: rating (B2-3), the outbox accelerator (B2-4), reconcile (B2-5); the first live run is the owner's step with the date X.

- **`billing.rate` (B2-3, `internal/platform/billing/rate`) writes ONE direct charge per billable event, and the reversal of a cancelled session is a PAIRED row, never an update.** The job (30 s, self-scheduling like collect, off until `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE` is set) lists events with no direct charge oldest first and rates each in its own transaction under `pg_advisory_xact_lock` on its SESSION (else the event), so a sale, the cancellation of its session and a second pass never interleave; `charges_one_direct_per_event_uq` is the backstop. Terms, price-list version and individual rate are those at the event's `occurred_at` (`billing.TermsAt` + `billing.Rate`), never at the time of the pass. A free ticket, a refund, a cancelled ticket and an invitation get a zero charge with a reason (`free`, `journal_only`), so "rated" always means "has a direct charge"; a charge no rule produced stores `rule_type = 'none'`. **Only `session_cancelled` reverses**: for every positive direct charge of the session's `ticket_sold` events with no reversal it writes a negative charge with `reversal_of`, the SAME `billable_event_id` as the original (so a period's sum nets to zero), plus a `charge_reversals` row whose cause is the cancellation; the cancellation event itself costs 0. It waits (stays unrated) while any `ticket_sold` event of the session is unrated, and a sale rated after its session's cancellation was processed (a late collect) is reversed at once. What happens to the money is block B3 (`Settler`: `Settle` after a positive direct charge, `SettleReversal` after a reversal, both inside the charge's transaction, `NopSettler` until then; the founders' 16.10 decision is that a cancelled session gives BONUS credits, capped at 30 % of the next payments, not purchased credits one to one). An event the job cannot rate stays unrated and is logged each pass with a warning: no usable terms at that date, a rule the engine cannot compute, the engine refusing the event, and a deferred charge (`fx_rate_missing`: a percentage rule on a sale in another currency than the account's, no exchange rate exists). A database failure stops the pass. A sale made AFTER its session's cancellation is neither waited for nor reversed (`...Until` queries, bounded by the cancellation's `occurred_at`); a journal-only event (refund, cancelled ticket, cancellation) is rated 0 even when the account's terms are unusable (`billing.ErrBadTerms`, `ErrNoTerms`, ...), so a cancellation can still reverse; a negative engine result is skipped, never written (`charges_direct_amount_check`). The unrated list is a KEYSET walk (`ListUnratedBillableEventsAfter`, 40 pages x 500 per pass), so skipped events are not re-read within a pass; more than ~20,000 permanently skipped events would still hide newer ones across passes. Known scaling limit: the unrated query scans `billable_events` by `occurred_at` and anti-joins `charges`, which is fine for tens of thousands of events; add a watermark on `collected_at` before it holds millions. An integration test must give the Rater a `Lister` scoped to its own organization (see `scopedLister`): the shared database holds other packages' events, and a charge pins its event with a foreign key.

- **The billing outbox leg (B2-4, `internal/platform/billing/accel`) is only an accelerator and must keep answering nil.** It reacts to `v1.order.paid`, `v1.ticket.refunded`, `v1.ticket.cancelled`, `v1.ticket.revoked`, `v1.session.cancelled`, `v1.event.published` by setting a one-slot flag; a goroutine (`Run`) then makes ONE extra collect-and-rate pass at most every 2 seconds, so a burst of events costs one pass. `Dispatch` touches no database and no network, never blocks, and returns nil for every event, relevant or not, known order or not, nil leg or not; a failed or panicking pass is logged and changes nothing for the other legs of the fan-out or for the outbox row. The scheduled `billing.collect` and `billing.rate` jobs stay the source of truth and find everything the leg missed (a dropped flag, a restart). Like both jobs it exists only once `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE` is set (`buildBilling` in `cmd/arena-worker/main.go` returns a nil rater otherwise, and a nil `*Accelerator` is an inert leg). It sits in the fan-out after the webhook legs, so it can neither delay them nor re-trigger them.

- **`billing.reconcile` (B2-5, `internal/platform/billing/reconcile`) is the daily read-only cross-check of the billing pipeline against the sales tables, and a finding is a fact to explain, never a thing to auto-repair.** Registered next to the other billing jobs only when `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE` is set (inert and never self-scheduling otherwise; first run 10 minutes after worker start, then every 24 h, next run scheduled BEFORE the work). From the date X up to a 1-hour grace before now it reports: `order_items_without_event` (a ticket line of a paid order has no `ticket_sold`/`ticket_complimentary` event), `tickets_without_event` (an active ticket of a paid order or an invitation has none; an order-level event with no ticket link also covers it), `events_without_charge` (collected more than 6 h ago and still no direct charge: no terms, rule not implemented, broken price list), `reversal_mismatch` (a reversal that is not exactly the negative of its charge in amount, currency, event or account), `collect_lag` (the oldest `collect.*` cursor older than 45 min, or no cursor at all while X is older than that) and `rate_lag` (the oldest unrated event collected more than 30 min ago). It writes ONLY metrics (`arena_billing_reconcile_findings{check}`, `arena_billing_lag_seconds{stage}`, set to 0 when clean so a gauge falls back) and ops alerts through `opswatchdog.AlertEngine.Sync` (fingerprint `billing_reconcile:<check>`, high for gaps, warn for lags, resolved by the next clean run). Alerts and reports carry counts, organization ids and ids of billing facts only: no buyer name, e-mail, phone or organization name. Money-guard consequence: nothing in `internal/platform/billing/**` may use a float, so lags are `int64` seconds (even the metrics method). **A deleted `billable_event` is NOT recreated by the next collect pass** (the source cursor is already past it): the repair is to delete that source's `billing_job_cursors` row (key `collect.<source>`) and let the pass re-read from the date X (events are idempotent by `(kind, subject_id)`), then let rate price it. The credit-balance checks of spec 41 §6.10 (`consume` = allocations, `remaining` = journal) wait for the credit tables of block B3. Run it by hand on a SCRATCH copy of a stand with `DATABASE_URL=… go run ./apps/backend/tools/billing-reconcile --since <X> [--collect --rate --copy]` (`--collect/--rate` WRITE events and charges and refuse to run without `--copy`; plain reconcile only reads). Dev-stand note: its paid orders end on 2026-09-22 and its migration head was 108, so a copy needs `arena-migrate up` first and a date X before the data (a run with X = 2026-09-01 over a copy: 24124 `ticket_sold` + 70 `event_published` events in 32 organizations, 24194 charges = 24124 x 59 EUR minor units + 70 zero `no_rule`, all six checks clean). Tests: `reconcile/reconcile_integration_test.go` (deleted line -> alert without personal data -> resolved after the repair; unrated and lag; reversal mismatch; collect lag), `job_test.go`.

- **The organizer sees what the platform charged them through two read-only routes under billing.read (B2-6, `hbilling/org_charges.go`, queries `gen/billing_read.sql.go`).** `GET /v1/organizations/{org_id}/billing/charges?from&to&event_id&limit&cursor` (keyset page, newest event first, limit 1-200 default 50, opaque `next_cursor`, `from` inclusive and `to` exclusive on the EVENT time, RFC 3339 or YYYY-MM-DD) and `GET .../billing/events/{event_id}/summary` (totals per currency, net per rule type = the lines of the future invoice, per session, events by kind). Every answer says `preliminary: true`: nothing is invoiced yet. Sums are PER CURRENCY over the whole filter, never one page and never across currencies. A reversal row belongs to the ORIGINAL event, so it carries that event's time and the charges of one event net to zero when it is reversed. Both routes carry `{org_id}`, so membership and `billing.read` are judged in the path organization by the gate (the owner holds it, the manager does not: 403), every query filters `billable_events.org_id`, and an `event_id` that is another organization's or unknown is the route's own 404 `billing.event_not_found` on both routes (`eventInOrg`; a soft-deleted event of the organization still answers, its charges outlive it). The summary route has a child id, so it sits in `orgIsolationAuditedSafe`; `billing_charges_integration_test.go` drives the owner, manager, a foreign owner, and a foreign API key. The legacy `GET .../billing/usage` keeps its shape and is now fed by the `billing.usage_projection` job (`internal/platform/billing/usage`, every 10 min, only when `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE` is set): it SETs `usage_records` for the previous and the current UTC month from `billable_events` counts (`ticket_sold`, `ticket_complimentary`, `event_published`, `ticket_refunded`) and touches a row only when a number changed. Months before the date X are partial by nature, and `IncrementBillingUsage` still has no callers.

- **The web screen "Billing → Usage" (B2-7, `apps/event-center/src/screens/BillingScreen.tsx`, route `/o/$orgId/billing`) is read-only and PRELIMINARY, decided by `can("billing.read")` from `GET .../me`.** The owner holds it, the manager and the agent do not: no menu entry, an explanation on the address, and NO billing request is sent (the live twin proves it with a request listener, and the API says 403). The month is a UTC calendar month; the totals per currency come from the API for the WHOLE month (`totals` of `GET .../billing/charges?from&to`), the "By event" table is counted from the charge rows already loaded and says so when more pages wait (`billing.events.partial`), and one event opens its API summary, which covers the whole life of the event in ALL months (a note says so; do not compare it with the month list). Money goes through `formatMoney` per currency and is never added across currencies (`lib/billing.ts`, `groupByEvent`). The plural-free phrasing `Charges: {count}` is deliberate (the catalogs have no plural engine). Twin test `tests/smoke/live/billing.spec.ts` reads facts the seed writes through `internal/platform/billing/e2eseed` (the ONLY place outside the engine that may insert billing rows, because the append-only guard allows inserts only under `internal/platform/billing/**`): three sales of the main event (one reversed) and one of `E2E Billing Second Show` in the current UTC month, one sale the day before it, 0.59 EUR each; the call is idempotent by fixed ids and the spec never writes, so there is no reset. A second event in the fixture organization is the cost of this twin: live specs that count events must filter by name.

- **Credits (B3-1/B3-2, migration 0159, `internal/platform/billing/credits`) are spent by the rating transaction, and a cancelled session turns what was PAID into one bonus lot, never back into the purchased lot.** `credit_lots` (purchased: a pack's tier rates snapshotted at the purchase; bonus: no tier) and the append-only `credit_ledger` (amount always positive, sign by `entry_type`, `remaining_after` snapshot) are written only by that package, always after `gen.LockBillingAccount` (FOR UPDATE of `billing_accounts`; lock order: the rate job's session lock, then the account). `credits.Settler` is wired into `billing.rate` by the worker: `Settle` spends lots available AT `occurred_at` (bought no later, not expired), the pure `MakePlan` (`plan.go`) decides: price = the first purchased lot's tier (unseated rate + pack surcharge for a seated ticket, each capped by the pay-as-you-go price; an individual rate pays packs at face; low price, venue % and per-event fees pay their own amount), bonus pays at most `bonus_share_cap` of the unseated part rounded down (never the surcharge unless `bonus_covers_surcharge`, never below `floor_per_ticket_minor`, no bonus on low price / venue), purchased lots FIFO with the leftover converted between tiers in proportion, what no lot covers stays for the invoice, and the rest is the `pack_rate_discount` allocation (never invoiced). A bonus lot never pays for the event its cancellation came from (`source_event_id`). `SettleReversal` (B-6 revised): purchased-lot and invoice-line allocations of the reversed charge go into ONE bonus lot per `session_cancelled` event (`source=cancellation`, `source_ref` = that event, `purchased_at` = its time, `expires_at` NULL until the owner names the term: ST-2); a bonus allocation goes back to its lot unless it expired (`reversal_dropped`); the uncovered part gives nothing (the reversal row nets it). `credits.Purchase` writes a lot + `purchase` entry idempotently by `(account, invoice)`; it must run in the transaction that marks the invoice paid. `billing.credit_expire` (hourly, only with `BILLING_COLLECT_SINCE`) empties expired lots with an `expire` entry; reminders wait for texts (ST-3). `billing.reconcile` gained `credit_journal_mismatch` (lot balance vs journal and last snapshot) and `credit_allocation_mismatch` (consume entries vs allocations). Test fixtures that delete billing rows delete `credit_ledger`, `charge_allocations`, then `credit_lots` (the allocation FK) before charges. `credits_integration_test.go` includes 50 parallel settles on one lot.

- **Platform invoices, credit packs and how their money arrives (B3-3, B4, migration 0160, `internal/platform/billing/invoicing`, `hbilling/account_billing.go`, `admin_invoices.go`, `billing_webhooks.go`).** An account invoice is an `invoices` row WITH `account_id` (legacy tariff invoices keep it NULL; the legacy routes refuse an account invoice with 409 `billing.account_invoice`, and `GetInvoiceByOrgAndPeriod` reads legacy rows only). Kinds `usage`/`credit_pack`; numbers are gapless per issuer and year (`invoice_number_series`, `<series or ARENA>-<year>-<seq 06d>`) and are given at ISSUE, never to a draft; every issued invoice has a payment reference `ARN-XXXX-XXXX` (`invoicing.FindReferences` reads it out of any bank text, case and separators free). **A card payer adds the average payment system fee as its own line** (`billing_payment_fees`, `invoicing.CardFee` grosses up so the platform nets the invoice; there is NO seeded fee: card payment is refused until the operator adds one through `/v1/admin/billing/payment-fees`, ST-2); a bank transfer or an operator-recorded payment may be the total OR the total without the card fee (net payment). `invoicing.MarkPaid` writes the credit lot of a pack in the SAME transaction (`credits.Purchase`), so an invoice is never paid without its credits; a repeat with the same reference is a no-op. A paid invoice is never voided (credit note), and a void goes to Stripe FIRST (`VoidInvoice` reads the Stripe invoice; already paid = 409 and the webhook books it). Usage invoices take every direct, non-reversed charge of a closed month (issuer zone) minus what lots and discounts covered, tie each charge to its line with an `invoice_line` allocation, and a void deletes those allocations so the next invoice takes them. Tax is not computed (`tax_minor` 0, ST-3). **The platform Stripe account is `BILLING_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` + `BILLING_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (arena-api only, never an organizer key)**; with the key set, `POST /v1/billing/stripe/webhook` is served by `AccountBillingHandler.HandleStripeWebhook` (otherwise the legacy receiver): a pack is paid through a Checkout Session, a usage invoice through a hosted Stripe invoice, both made lazily with idempotency keys derived from the invoice; the event is booked and recorded in `billing_stripe_events` in ONE transaction (a redelivery is a no-op, a database failure answers 500 so Stripe redelivers, `checkout.session.completed` with `payment_status` other than `paid` is not recorded). The endpoint follows `stripeBaseURL()` (Options override, then `STRIPE_API_BASE_URL`), so tests stub it. **Bank feed:** `POST /v1/billing/bank/webhook/{provider}` with `X-Arena-Signature: sha256=<hex HMAC of the raw body>` keyed by `BILLING_BANK_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, 1-500 transfers, stored once per (provider, external id), one transaction per transfer, matched by reference AND amount; anything else waits in `/v1/admin/billing/bank-transactions` with `unmatched_reason` (`no_reference`, `unknown_reference`, `amount_mismatch`, `currency_mismatch`, `invoice_not_open`); provider `manual` is the operator's import. **fee_mode on terms:** `organizer_pays` (default) or `buyer_pays`: then a sold ticket's charge is the service charge the buyer paid (`rule_type` `buyer_service_charge`, reason `buyer_pays`, the price-list rate kept as `inputs.standard_minor`); `GET .../billing/service-fee-quote` tells the organizer the service charge per ticket that covers the platform (`billing.StandardTicketCharge`, the rating code itself). Applying such a service charge automatically at checkout is NOT built. Organizer routes: `balance`, `packs`, `packs/{pack_id}/order` (Idempotency-Key required, `billing.purchase`, owner only), `account-invoices[/{invoice_id}[/pay-by-card]]`, `ledger`, `payer`, `service-fee-quote`. A draft is never shown to the organizer. Test external ids (bank transfer ids, Stripe event ids) must be random per run: UUIDv7 prefixes repeat within minutes and a fixed-prefix id hits the (provider, external id) dedup of a previous run.
